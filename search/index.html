Nabi Search

One search for the whole library: people and places with their Hebrew, Greek and meanings, topics, patterns, every word of the KJV, the scrolls, and the rest of the site.

Names, places and references: STEPBible (CC BY 4.0), reformatted, with some short notes adapted by its makers from AI output. Verses: King James Version. Hebrew and Greek: Strong’s, 1890 (Open Scriptures, CC BY-SA). Meanings: Hitchcock’s Bible Names, 1869. Topics: Nave’s Topical Bible, 1896 (text via Brady Stephenson’s Bible Data, CC BY 4.0). Portraits: Nabi Portrait Collection, AI-assisted. 1 Enoch: R. H. Charles. "Connected" means tagged in the scroll or named in its title. "Mentions this" means the scroll uses the name at least three times, by automatic count, so it may refer to a different person with the same name. Only public titles appear here, never scroll text. Searches are logged by word only, with no name or address.

We keep the words you search and, if you ask to be told when a scroll is out, your email. We use them to decide what to write next and to send that one notice. We do not sell them. Questions: info@thenabiglobal.com.
