Library of Ancient Texts

A house of many rooms. Walk in, pick a shelf, and read for free at the public archive that holds it.

Every link goes to an outside public archive, such as Internet Archive, Sefaria, Early Jewish Writings or Project Gutenberg. School of the Nabi does not host these texts and does not control those sites. Older translations are often used because they are free, and scholarship has moved on in places. Listing a text is not an endorsement of what it teaches. Room paintings are AI-assisted illustrations.
