The Fifteen Officers of the Later Temple

Mishnah Shekalim 5:1. The Mishnah names them by office. They did not all serve at the same time.

Johanan son of PhinehasThe seals
AhijahThe drink offerings
Mattithiah son of SamuelThe lots
PethahiahThe bird offerings
Ben AhijahPriests sick in the bowels
NehuniahThe water ditches and wells
GabiniThe herald
Ben GeberThe gates and their guards
Ben BabiThe guard, or the wicks (translations differ)
Ben ArzaThe cymbal
Hugros son of LeviThe singing
House of GarmuThe shewbread
House of AbtinasThe incense
EleazarThe curtains
PhinehasThe vestments

Scripture text is the KJV. The Jewish sources show how Second Temple and rabbinic writers saw the service. Most were written after the Temple fell, so they may describe later practice or ideal memory, not David’s day. Chronicles credits David with the courses; some modern scholars date the 24-course system to the Second Temple period.
