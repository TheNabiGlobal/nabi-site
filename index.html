01 · The House

A house of Scripture and remembrance.

A gathering point for those who seek understanding of the Word in their time.

The School of the Nabi is a house of Scripture dedicated to deep biblical study, prophetic understanding, and the recovery of the full testimony of the Word of God.

We embody the spirit of the mighty men of Issachar—those who understood the times and knew what Israel ought to do (1 Chronicles 12:32). With the applied hearts of Daniel, mouths formed in the similitude of the prophets, and the daily prayer of Solomon on behalf of God’s people, the School of the Nabi exists to recover what was buried, restore what was lost, and return the Word to those who hunger for it.

Through prophetic scrolls, structured teaching series, and deep historical research, we trace the unbroken thread of the Living Word across the entire biblical narrative—from Genesis to Revelation—so that believers may see its unity, depth, and living power.

Nabi (נָבִיא) is the Hebrew word for prophet. School of the Nabi carries on the pattern of the “sons of the prophets” in Samuel and Kings: a house of study, teaching, and song. See 1 Samuel 10:5.
